YouTube Background playerWebsiteGithub

I got tired of YouTube pausing my videos when I turned off my phone's screen. This tool allows me to play videos with my screen off. Also allows linking playlists and saving them to LocalStorage for quick access.

C boilerplate generatorWebsiteGithub

Small tool to generate boilerplate C code and compilation commands. Also has settings profiles.

Cheatsheet generatorWebsiteGithub

Web-based tool to create cheatsheets as I'm taking notes during class, and exports them to PNG. Also allows multiple profiles via JSON config, corresponding to the different subjects I'm taking notes on.

VinderGithub

Logs into Vinted (second-hand clothes & electronics) with my credentials and creates a Tinder-style feed. I can "like" products in this feed to add them to my account's favorites, or make the lowest allowed offer to the seller, directly from the UI.

Pocket FolderGithub

macOS application that sits in the menu bar, and allows quick access (and drag/drop) to selected folders.
